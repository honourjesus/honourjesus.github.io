Community & Leadership

My commitment to AI accessibility extends beyond research to community building, mentorship, and grassroots AI literacy. Over five years, I have built and led organisations, mentored hundreds of students, and organised events reaching thousands across Nigeria and beyond.

Organised workshops, hackathons, and the DSN AI Invasion training programme, facilitating Python and data science instruction to 100+ students and professionals.
