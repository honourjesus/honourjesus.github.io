Beyond Translation: Typology-Aware Speech Adaptation for Hausa in High-Stakes Digital Assessment

Bezaleel, H.-J.

Manuscript under review. Addresses the unique phonological and tonal structure of Hausa and proposes typology-aware adaptation pipelines for deploying speech models in digital educational assessments. Self-Training with Confidence-Based Filtering for African ASR at Scale

Bezaleel, H.-J.

Designs a multi-signal pseudo-label filtering framework combining decoder log-probability, CTC–decoder agreement (via an auxiliary CTC head on the Whisper encoder), length-ratio constraints, and language-ID confidence. Retains only samples passing all four gates. Full ablation study across seven filter combinations and four unlabelled data scales (25h–200h per language) isolates per-signal contribution and identifies filtering saturation points.

Why Large Language Models Fail for Hausa Educational Content: Cascading Errors From Translation to Speech to Comprehension

Bezaleel, H.-J. (2026)

Presents a systematic failure analysis of end-to-end LLM pipelines for Hausa educational content. Traces how semantic omission in machine translation, lexical diversity collapse (TTR: 17.3% → 13.9%), LLM hallucination, and 100% tonal error rates in baseline ASR models cascade through the pipeline, reducing reasoning accuracy from 82% to under 30% on downstream tasks.

Bridging Language Barriers in Education Using TTS–ASR Approaches for Low-Resource Languages in Nigeria

Bezaleel, H.-J. (2025)

Poster presentation. Develops and evaluates end-to-end TTS–ASR pipelines for Nigerian low-resource languages, validated on the HausaPQ-Speech corpus (1,000 human-validated samples). Reports WER variance of 68–92% for baseline models and establishes evaluation benchmarks using WER, CER, and MOS metrics.
